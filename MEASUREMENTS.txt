11) PROMPT INJECTION DEFENSE (OWASP LLM01) — VERIFIED 2026-07-27
================================================================================

Threat model:
  WebScraperPlugin fetches attacker-controlled pages. That text enters chat
  history of an LLM with auto function calling over WebSearch, WebScraper, and
  VectorMemory. A hostile page can attempt to:
    (a) override the system prompt / change the agent's goal
    (b) call VectorMemory.Save to poison future retrievals
